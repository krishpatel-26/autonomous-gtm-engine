# Campaign release checklist

Before activation:
- Confirm target segment and legitimate business purpose.
- Verify contact source, freshness, and permitted use.
- Deduplicate prospects and apply suppression lists.
- Review claims, personalization, and sender identity.
- Confirm required consent/legal basis for the relevant jurisdiction and channel.
- Require explicit human approval for outreach.
- Set daily volume limits and stop conditions.
- Provide a clear opt-out path and honor it promptly.
- Monitor bounce, complaint, reply, and conversion signals.
- Pause when a quality or safety threshold is breached.

The project currently prepares plans and drafts; do not assume external messages are sent.
