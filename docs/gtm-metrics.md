# GTM metrics definitions

- Lead acceptance rate: accepted leads divided by leads reviewed.
- Duplicate rate: duplicate prospects divided by prospects ingested.
- Approval rate: approved drafts divided by drafts submitted for review.
- Bounce rate: bounced deliveries divided by attempted deliveries, if delivery integration exists.
- Reply rate: replies divided by delivered messages, if tracked with appropriate permissions.
- Conversion rate: defined conversions divided by the agreed eligible denominator.

Document each denominator, time window, and source. Never present synthetic demo metrics as real campaign performance. Avoid optimizing for volume at the expense of relevance, consent, or complaint rates.
